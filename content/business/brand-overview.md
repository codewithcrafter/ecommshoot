## Brand Purpose & Core Philosophy
Ecomshoot was founded to bridge the gap between slow, prohibitive commercial advertising studios and low-quality catalog cutout mills. We engineer high-performance visual assets designed specifically to increase click-through rates, drive add-to-cart actions, and eliminate product returns for e-commerce brands.

## The Ecomshoot Technical Pillars

### 1. Optical Acuity & Medium Format Capture
We shoot on 100+ megapixel medium format systems (High-resolution commercial digital systems) and high-resolution Sony full-frame sensors paired with specialized calibrated commercial prime and prime tilt-shift optics. This delivers unmatched resolution, allowing digital shoppers to inspect microscopic fabric weaves and fine product finishes.

### 2. Spectral Color Fidelity (ΔE < 1.0)
Inaccurate product color is the single largest driver of e-commerce returns. We photograph custom X-Rite ColorChecker charts under calibrated 5600K Calibrated studio strobe illumination to construct proprietary ICC color profiles for every shoot session.

### 3. Zero AI Hallucination Guarantee
We capture authentic physical products under true optical glass. We never use generative AI diffusion models to fabricate product geometry or synthetic surfaces that mislead buyers.

### 4. 100% Hand-Crafted Post-Production
Automated cutout algorithms leave telltale jagged halos and washed-out edges. Every Ecomshoot asset is clipped using hand-drawn vector paths by senior retouchers, paired with frequency separation and natural contact shadow reconstruction.
