# About Ecomshoot - Artistry Meets E-Commerce Conversion Science

> Founded by seasoned commercial photographers and e-commerce strategists, Ecomshoot bridges the gap between artistic commercial photography and high-conversion catalog assets.

## Our Mission
To provide high-growth brands with visual assets that dramatically reduce returns, increase average order value (AOV), and establish undeniable category dominance.

## The Ecomshoot Quality Standard
1. **Color Accuracy Guarantee**: Every setup utilizes X-Rite ColorChecker charts and custom ICC profiles to ensure online images match physical merchandise 100%.
2. **Defect-Free Cleanliness**: Products undergo ultrasonic cleaning, anti-static dusting, and fabric steaming prior to being placed before the lens.
3. **Marketplace Compliance**: 100% compliant with Amazon Seller Central, Walmart Marketplace, Google Merchant Center, and Shopify Plus specifications.
