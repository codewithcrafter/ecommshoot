# Studio Intake, Sample Shipping & Production Booking Guidelines

> Step-by-step instructions for booking studio production runs and safely dispatching merchandise samples.

## Step 1: Request Rate Card & Production Availability
Submit your SKU count, service requirements, and desired delivery timeframe through our [Online Booking Form](http://127.0.0.1:8000/contact).

## Step 2: Shipping Sample Merchandise
- Our intake department provides a pre-addressed shipping tag and unique Production Batch ID.
- Securely pack samples with protective bubble wrap.
- Include a printed copy of your Packing Manifest inside the parcel.

## Step 3: Studio Shoot & Real-Time Review
- Upon parcel arrival, items are logged into secure climate-controlled lockers.
- You receive a private digital proofing gallery link to review unretouched captures in real-time.

## Step 4: High-Resolution Delivery & Sample Return
- Master TIFF, high-res JPEG, and web-optimized WebP files delivered via secure cloud link.
- Samples can be safely returned via your preferred shipping courier or donated per your written authorization.
