## Section 4: Sample Shipment & Logistics Guidelines

1. **Packaging**: Send items in original retail packaging with protective exterior boxing.
2. **Sample Selection**: Inspect samples beforehand for surface blemishes, scratches, or manufacturing flaws. Send at least 2 units for hero items when possible.
3. **Labeling**: Include a packing slip with Brand Name, Contact Person, SKU Count, and Special Handling Instructions.
4. **Return Logistics**: After shooting and approval, products are carefully re-packed and returned via prepaid courier or made available for local pickup.
