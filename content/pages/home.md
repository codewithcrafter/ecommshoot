# Ecomshoot - Visual Assets Engineered to Multiply E-Commerce Conversions

> Premier commercial product photography studio and post-production house serving direct-to-consumer (D2C) brands, high-growth Shopify merchants, Amazon enterprise sellers, and international fashion retailers.

## Studio Core Disciplines
- **Studio Packshots**: Pure RGB 255,255,255 white background catalog photography passing marketplace compliance on first upload.
- **Lifestyle Editorial Staging**: Authentic context imagery captured on custom-built indoor and outdoor stage sets.
- **Apparel & Fashion**: Ghost mannequin 3D hollow-body captures, pin-sharp flat lays, and live-model lookbooks.
- **Micro-Detailed Jewellery**: 150MP macro focus-stacked imagery rendering diamond scintillation and precious metal finish.
- **Commercial Post-Production**: Hand-drawn vector clipping paths, frequency separation retouching, and ICC color calibration.

## Studio Facility & Technology
- **Camera Systems**: Phase One IQ4 150MP Medium Format, Sony Alpha 61MP Full-Frame.
- **Optics**: Schneider Kreuznach prime lenses, Canon L-Series tilt-shift lenses.
- **Lighting**: Broncolor digital studio strobes with calibrated color temperature consistency across all flash power outputs.
- **Turnaround**: Standard 5-7 business days with 48-hour rush options available.
