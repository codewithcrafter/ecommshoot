## Transparent Commercial Rate Structure

Ecomshoot operates on transparent, volume-tiered rate schedules tailored to catalog scope and creative requirements. We do not charge hidden studio equipment rental fees or usage royalties.

### 1. Catalog & Packshot Production (Per-Image / Per-SKU)
- **Standard White Background Packshot**: Pure RGB #255 background, hand-drawn clipping path, natural contact shadow, Amazon/Shopify compliance.
- **Volume Tiers**:
  - Tier 1: 10 – 49 Images
  - Tier 2: 50 – 199 Images (Tiered Volume Discount)
  - Tier 3: 200+ Images (Enterprise Production Rate Card)

### 2. Specialized Macro & Jewellery Photography
- **Optical Stacking**: Includes 20 to 60 slice automated focus stacking rail capture, refraction correction, diamond sparkle enhancement, and gold/silver tone balancing.

### 3. Styled Lifestyle & In-Context Sets
- **Editorial Production**: Includes prop sourcing, background surface styling, propped set construction, and commercial art direction.

### 4. Creative Advertising Campaigns
- **Concept Shoots**: Includes custom set construction, kinetic splash freezing, liquid droplets, levitation rigs, and high-end advertising composites.
