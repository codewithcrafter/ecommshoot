---

## Stage 01: Pre-Production & Creative Briefing
- **Client Onboarding**: Review brand guidelines, typography standards, target sales channels (Amazon, Shopify, DTC, Instagram ads).
- **Shot List Architecture**: Construct an itemized shot list categorizing each SKU by angle (Hero 0°, 45° isometric, profile, top-down flat lay, macro detail, packaging).
- **Prop & Mood Board Sourcing**: For lifestyle and creative campaigns, our art directors curate tactile props, organic surfaces (stone, marble, aged wood, brushed linen), and custom color palettes.

## Stage 02: Logistics, Ingestion & Product Prep
- **Intake Logging**: Products arrive at our Delhi facility, barcodes scanned, and stored in secure climate-controlled staging bays.
- **Condition Audit**: Under calibrated 5000K daylight lighting, each sample is inspected for scratches or defects.
- **Preparation & Styling**: Garments are steamed with industrial steamers; jewellery is ultrasonically cleaned; glass and acrylic packaging are treated with anti-static ionizing brushes.

## Stage 03: The Studio Shoot & Tethered Capture
- **Lighting Architecture**: Tailored lighting modifiers (Calibrated studio reflectors, softboxes, cross-polarized diffusers) are dialed in according to surface reflectivity.
- **Medium Format Capture**: Products are captured on High-resolution commercial digital systems digital backs or Sony 61MP bodies tethered directly to Capture One Pro.
- **Color Calibration**: X-Rite ColorChecker Digital SG targets are photographed under the exact lighting setup to generate custom ICC profiles ensuring ΔE < 1.0 color accuracy.
- **Live Client Proofing**: Remote clients receive live web gallery links to review and approve captures in real time.

## Stage 04: Master Post-Production & Digital Retouching
- **100% Vector Pen Tool Clipping**: Products are extracted using meticulous hand-drawn bezier paths (no automated AI fuzzy cutouts).
- **Surface Cleanup**: Dust specks, lint, micro-scratches, and packaging seams are removed while preserving genuine material texture.
- **Frequency Separation**: Complex textures (fabrics, cosmetics, leather) are retouched using dual-frequency separation to smooth blemishes without blurring authentic weave.
- **Drop Shadow Crafting**: Realistic contact shadows and ambient occlusion drop shadows are hand-rendered to anchor the product naturally.

## Stage 05: Multi-Format Handoff & Cloud Delivery
- **Export Standards**: Assets are exported in multiple resolutions:
  - High-res Master TIFF (16-bit, ProPhoto RGB or Adobe RGB) for print and archiving.
  - Full-res JPEG (3000x3000px, 300 DPI, sRGB) for marketplace catalogs.
  - Compressed WebP/JPEG for fast-loading e-commerce web storefronts.
- **Delivery Platform**: Organized cloud storage with permanent download links provided to the client team.
