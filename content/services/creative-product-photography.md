## Section 6: Step-by-Step Production Process

### Heading (H2)
**Our Structured Studio Workflow**

### Context
We work with a clear process that will take you from initial call to final delivery.

### Workflow Sequence
01. **Concept Pitch & Visual Treatment**: Our creative director develops 3 distinct visual treatments with sketches, color swatches, ingredient lists, and lighting diagrams.
02. **Physical Set Construction & Prop Fabrication**: Our artisan makers cut acrylics, curate authentic botanicals, and build custom rigs to suspend components securely.
03. **High-Speed Capture & Lighting Direction**: Using Calibrated studio strobes power packs with ultra-short flash duration, we trigger synchronized bursts capturing split-second movement.
04. **Fine Art Composite & Retouching**: We combine multi-exposure lighting brackets, blend optimal splash formations, and paint in hand-crafted shadow gradations.

## Section 7: Frequently Asked Questions

### Context


### Question 1: Can you create realistic liquid splash and drip effects?
**Answer**: Absolutely. We specialize in high-speed splash photography using physical liquids, glycerin droplets for lasting dew, and synchronized flash triggers to freeze movement with tack-sharp clarity.

### Question 2: How involved can our team be in the creative direction?
**Answer**: You can be as hands-on or hands-off as you prefer! We can develop complete concepts from scratch for your approval, or execute your agency moodboard down to the exact millimeter.
