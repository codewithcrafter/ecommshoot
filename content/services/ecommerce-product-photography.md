02. **Assembly-Line Studio Staging**: Products move through specialized stations: unboxing, lint removal, ultrasonic cleaning, staging, and precision mounting.

## Section 7: Frequently Asked Questions

### Context


### Question 1: How do you ensure lighting remains identical when we send products months apart?
**Answer**: Every client has a proprietary Digital Lighting Recipe document recording exact strobe power settings, light modifier distances, focal lengths, camera heights, and color balance profiles.

### Question 2: Can you handle hundreds of color variations?
**Answer**: Yes, our production facility features automated batch workflows designed specifically for high-count SKU matrices and seasonal collection drops.

### Question 3: Do you deliver files with custom SKU filenames?
**Answer**: Yes! Simply provide your SKU spreadsheet, and our asset management script renames all final renders to your exact specification (e.g., SKU_01_HERO.webp, SKU_02_ANGLE.webp).

### Question 4: Are these photos ready for Shopify and Amazon?
**Answer**: Every deliverable is pre-scaled to square 2000x2000px or 3000x3000px at 72DPI with sRGB color space, ready for immediate platform upload.
