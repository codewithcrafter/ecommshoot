### Step 2: Assembly-Line Studio Staging
Products move through specialized stations: unboxing, lint removal, ultrasonic cleaning, staging, and precision mounting.

### Step 3: Calibrated Batch Photography
Rig-locked camera axes guarantee that whether SKU #1 or SKU #500 is in the bay, framing and lighting match precisely.

### Step 4: Automated Delivery & Cloud Ingestion
Assets are formatted, renamed to your exact SKU parameters, compressed for instant web loading, and uploaded to your cloud storage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you ensure lighting remains identical when we send products months apart?
Every client has a proprietary Digital Lighting Recipe document recording exact strobe power settings, light modifier distances, focal lengths, camera heights, and color balance profiles.

### Can you handle hundreds of color variations?
Yes, our production facility features automated batch workflows designed specifically for high-count SKU matrices and seasonal collection drops.

### Do you deliver files with custom SKU filenames?
Yes! Simply provide your SKU spreadsheet, and our asset management script renames all final renders to your exact specification (e.g., SKU_01_HERO.webp, SKU_02_ANGLE.webp).

### Are these photos ready for Shopify and Amazon?
Every deliverable is pre-scaled to square 2000x2000px or 3000x3000px at 72DPI with sRGB color space, ready for immediate platform upload.
