## Section 6: Step-by-Step Production Process

### Heading (H2)
**Process**

### Context
Our process is simple, with clear milestones from the day your samples arrive. This is how your fashion product photography stays consistent from one shoot to the next.

### Workflow Sequence
01. **Steam, Prep & Garment Inspection**: Every garment is professionally steamed, lint-rolled, inspected for loose threads, and prepared with precision dressmaker pins.
02. **Silhouette Sculpting & Fitting**: Garments are fitted to specialized modular mannequins or live talent, ensuring natural contouring and zero tension bunching.
03. **Softbox & Rim Light Calibration**: We craft diffuse key lighting paired with delicate rim highlights that carve out the fabric texture from the backdrop.
04. **Composite Stitching & Color Alignment**: Our digital artists composite ghost necklines, remove support pins, smooth minor fabric tension lines, and calibrate textile color.

## Section 7: Frequently Asked Questions

### Context


### Question 1: What is the difference between ghost mannequin and flat lay?
**Answer**: Ghost mannequin gives garments a hollow-man 3D shape as if worn by an invisible person, ideal for jackets, blazers, and shirts. Flat lay presents garments styled flat on a table, ideal for tees, casual knitwear, and accessories.

### Question 2: Can we hire diverse models through Ecomshoot?
**Answer**: Yes, we have relationships with premier model agencies representing diverse ethnicities, body types, and aesthetics. We manage castings, bookings, and release agreements.

### Question 3: How do you handle difficult reflective fabrics like satin or vinyl?
**Answer**: Our studio uses polarized lighting filters and large scrim diffusers that control harsh highlights while maintaining the lustrous character of satin, velvet, and coated technical materials.

### Question 4: Do you steam the garments prior to photography?
**Answer**: Yes! Professional industrial-grade fabric steaming and lint preparation are standard parts of our intake process at no extra fee.
