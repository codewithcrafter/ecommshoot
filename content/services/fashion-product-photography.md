# Runway-Grade Fashion & Apparel Photography for Contemporary Clothing Labels

> From architectural ghost mannequin executions and pin-sharp flat lays to dynamic live-model editorial lookbooks, we present fashion with elegance and drape integrity.

## Service Overview

Apparel is one of the most demanding categories in digital commerce. Fabrics shift, wrinkles appear under unflattering light, colors drift between silk and denim, and consumers demand to understand the exact drape, hemline movement, and fit before committing to purchase. Ecomshoot brings editorial runway discipline to commercial apparel photography, ensuring your silhouettes look sculpted and luxurious.

## Key Benefits & Commercial Impact

### Accurate Fit & Fabric Drape
Showcase real textile weight, seam tension, and tailored fit so shoppers can visualize how the garment wears on their body.

### 3D Depth with Ghost Mannequin
Present garments with invisible 3D volume, revealing interior linings, neck labels, and structural seams without distracting mannequins.

### High Garment Turnaround
High-throughput steaming, pin-dressing, and lighting configurations designed for seasonal fashion collection rollouts.

## Production Workflow

### Step 1: Steam, Prep & Garment Inspection
Every garment is professionally steamed, lint-rolled, inspected for loose threads, and prepared with precision dressmaker pins.

### Step 2: Silhouette Sculpting & Fitting
Garments are fitted to specialized modular mannequins or live talent, ensuring natural contouring and zero tension bunching.

### Step 3: Softbox & Rim Light Calibration
We craft diffuse key lighting paired with delicate rim highlights that carve out the fabric texture from the backdrop.

### Step 4: Composite Stitching & Color Alignment
Our digital artists composite ghost necklines, remove support pins, smooth minor fabric tension lines, and calibrate textile color.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between ghost mannequin and flat lay?
Ghost mannequin gives garments a hollow-man 3D shape as if worn by an invisible person, ideal for jackets, blazers, and shirts. Flat lay presents garments styled flat on a table, ideal for tees, casual knitwear, and accessories.

### Can we hire diverse models through Ecomshoot?
Yes, we have relationships with premier model agencies representing diverse ethnicities, body types, and aesthetics. We manage castings, bookings, and release agreements.

### How do you handle difficult reflective fabrics like satin or vinyl?
Our studio uses polarized lighting filters and large scrim diffusers that control harsh highlights while maintaining the lustrous character of satin, velvet, and coated technical materials.

### Do you steam the garments prior to photography?
Yes! Professional industrial-grade fabric steaming and lint preparation are standard parts of our intake process at no extra fee.
