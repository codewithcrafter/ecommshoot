### Heading (H2)
**Fine Jewellery Product Photography Services in Delhi**

### Detailed Description
Fine jewellery and luxury timepieces represent the pinnacle of commercial photographic difficulty. Highly reflective metals act like mirrors, diamonds scatter light into fiery chromatic aberrations, and miniature proportions require microscopic depth of field. At Ecomshoot, our dedicated luxury studio is equipped with customized diffusers, motorized focus rails, and cleanroom air filters that eliminate the dust particles invisible to the human eye.

### Studio Follow-up

## Section 7: Frequently Asked Questions

### Context


### Question 1: Why is focus stacking necessary for jewellery?
**Answer**: At macro magnification, the physical depth of field of even an f/16 lens is less than 1 millimeter. Focus stacking captures dozens of images across the product depth and stitches them into a single frame where every detail is in focus.

### Question 2: How do you secure valuable high-ticket merchandise?
**Answer**: Our studio maintains biometric security, 24/7 video surveillance, a dual-combination safe, and comprehensive commercial inland marine insurance covering valuable goods in transit and on premises.

### Question 3: Can you accurately capture the difference between 14k and 18k yellow gold?
**Answer**: Yes, our color-managed pipeline uses custom ICC camera calibration profiles that capture subtle differences between gold karat purities and rose gold alloys.

### Question 4: Do you shoot 360-degree interactive jewellery spins?
**Answer**: Yes, we provide 360-degree spin photography captured on motorized indexing tables, outputting smooth HTML5/GIF interactive widgets.
