# Micro-Precision Luxury & Fine Jewellery Photography Capturing Diamond Fire and Precious Sheen

> Specialized macro focus-stacking photography designed to eliminate unwanted reflections, reveal diamond facet scintillation, and render precious metals with breathtaking brilliance.

## Service Overview

Fine jewellery and luxury timepieces represent the pinnacle of commercial photographic difficulty. Highly reflective metals act like mirrors, diamonds scatter light into fiery chromatic aberrations, and miniature proportions require microscopic depth of field. At Ecomshoot, our dedicated luxury studio is equipped with customized diffusers, motorized focus rails, and cleanroom air filters that eliminate the dust particles invisible to the human eye.

## Key Benefits & Commercial Impact

### Sublime Diamond Scintillation
Controlled sparklers and polarized light reveal diamond fire, crystal facet clarity, and internal brilliance without muddying shadows.

### True Metallic Lustre
18K yellow gold, rose gold, platinum, and sterling silver rendered with accurate warmth, mirror sheen, and zero color contamination.

### Edge-to-Edge Razor Sharpness
Automated 30-to-80 frame focus-stacking composites ensure the entire ring band, prong, and gemstone are in tack-sharp focus.

## Production Workflow

### Step 1: Cleanroom Ultrasonic Preparation
Jewellery is immersed in ultrasonic cleaners, wiped with lint-free optical microfiber, and handled exclusively with anti-static gloves.

### Step 2: Precision Rigging & Articulation
Rings and pendants are mounted on micro-thin invisible monofilament or proprietary museum-grade mounting putty.

### Step 3: Motorized 50-Slice Focus Stacking
Our tethered macro lens records 30 to 80 consecutive exposures as the rail shifts focus from the front stone tip to the rear band.

### Step 4: Focus Stacking & High-End Retouching
Helicon Focus compiles the sharpest pixels from every slice; our senior retouchers hand-polish every facet and contour.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is focus stacking necessary for jewellery?
At macro magnification, the physical depth of field of even an f/16 lens is less than 1 millimeter. Focus stacking captures dozens of images across the product depth and stitches them into a single frame where every detail is in focus.

### How do you secure valuable high-ticket merchandise?
Our studio maintains biometric security, 24/7 video surveillance, a dual-combination safe, and comprehensive commercial inland marine insurance covering valuable goods in transit and on premises.

### Can you accurately capture the difference between 14k and 18k yellow gold?
Yes, our color-managed pipeline uses custom ICC camera calibration profiles that capture subtle differences between gold karat purities and rose gold alloys.

### Do you shoot 360-degree interactive jewellery spins?
Yes, we provide 360-degree spin photography captured on motorized indexing tables, outputting smooth HTML5/GIF interactive widgets.
