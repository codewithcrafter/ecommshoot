# Commercial Product Photo Editing, Clipping & High-End Digital Retouching

> Transform raw studio captures into museum-quality digital assets with pixel-perfect clipping paths, frequency separation, scratch removal, and master color balancing.

## Service Overview

Behind every breath-taking commercial image lies world-class digital post-production. Even the most carefully managed studio shoot encounters microscopic manufacturing imperfections, dust particles, uneven fabric seams, or minor packaging dents. Ecomshoot operates an in-house team of master digital retouchers who elevate good photographs into extraordinary commercial assets that sell.

## Key Benefits & Commercial Impact

### Flawless Surface Perfection
Eliminate manufacturing blemishes, seam glue, scratches, and micro-dust while preserving the authentic natural texture.

### 100% Hand-Drawn Clipping Paths
Precise pen-tool vector paths ensuring crisp, razor-sharp edges without the jagged halo artifacts common in automated AI cutouts.

### Accurate Color Correction
Calibrate digital color values against Pantone swatches and physical samples for flawless cross-screen color accuracy.

## Technical Specifications

- **Advanced Frequency Separation**: Separates color tone from high-frequency surface texture, allowing smooth lighting adjustments without blurring details.
- **Dynamic Multi-Shadow Creation**: Hand-crafted drop shadows, ambient contact shadows, and realistic surface reflections tailored to match client guidelines.
- **Ghost Mannequin Neck Jointing**: Seamlessly joining front and back interior garment collar photographs into a clean, uniform 3D apparel presentation.
- **Label & Artwork Replacement**: Digitally swapping language labels, correcting foil stamping alignment, or updating packaging claims without reshooting.

## Production Workflow

### Step 1: Raw Conversion & Color Profiling
Tethered 16-bit RAW captures are converted in Capture One with custom tone curves that maximize dynamic range preservation.

### Step 2: Pen Tool Vector Clipping & Masking
Retouchers draft hand-drawn vector paths along every product edge, ensuring smooth transitions even on intricate shapes.

### Step 3: Surface Cleanup & Texture Sculpting
Using frequency separation and cloning, we hand-clean every surface artifact while safeguarding authentic material grain.

### Step 4: Shadow Rendering & Multi-Format Export
Custom ambient contact shadows are applied, followed by automated multi-resolution web exports and QA checklist signoff.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do you use automated AI background removal tools?
Never for client deliverables. While AI tools are improving, they produce soft, blurry, or jagged edges and clip crucial product fringes. We hand-draw vector clipping paths using Photoshop Pen Tool for museum-grade precision.

### Can you edit photos that our team shot in-house?
Yes! Many brands send us their existing in-house RAW or TIFF image catalogs for color grading, background removal, and high-end retouching.

### Can you change product colors digitally to avoid shooting every variation?
Yes, our colorway alteration service allows you to shoot one hero SKU and generate 20 photorealistic colorway variants, saving thousands in production costs.

### What formats do you deliver?
We deliver layered PSD files (upon request), transparent PNGs, high-resolution TIFFs for print, and optimized WebP/JPEG files for web.
