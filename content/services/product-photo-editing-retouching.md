## Section 6: Step-by-Step Production Process

### Heading (H2)
**How We Work**

### Context
Our product photo editing services follow a clear process. Each stage has milestones to ensure you know where your images are.

### Workflow Sequence
01. **Raw Conversion & Color Profiling**: Tethered 16-bit RAW captures are converted in Capture One with custom tone curves that maximize dynamic range preservation.
02. **Pen Tool Vector Clipping & Masking**: Retouchers draft hand-drawn vector paths along every product edge, ensuring smooth transitions even on intricate shapes.
03. **Surface Cleanup & Texture Sculpting**: Using frequency separation and cloning, we hand-clean every surface artifact while safeguarding authentic material grain.
04. **Shadow Rendering & Multi-Format Export**: Custom ambient contact shadows are applied, followed by automated multi-resolution web exports and QA checklist signoff.

## Section 7: Frequently Asked Questions

### Context
Here are some quick questions about our product photo editing services.

### Question 1: Do you use automated AI background removal tools?
**Answer**: Never for client deliverables. While AI tools are improving, they produce soft, blurry, or jagged edges and clip crucial product fringes. We hand-draw vector clipping paths using Photoshop Pen Tool for museum-grade precision.

### Question 2: Can you edit photos that our team shot in-house?
**Answer**: Yes! Many brands send us their existing in-house RAW or TIFF image catalogs for color grading, background removal, and high-end retouching.

### Question 3: Can you change product colors digitally to avoid shooting every variation?
**Answer**: Yes, our colorway alteration service allows you to shoot one hero SKU and generate 20 photorealistic colorway variants, saving thousands in production costs.

### Question 4: What formats do you deliver?
**Answer**: We deliver layered PSD files (upon request), transparent PNGs, high-resolution TIFFs for print, and optimized WebP/JPEG files for web.
