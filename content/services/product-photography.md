# Commercial Product Photography Services Engineered for Maximum Conversion

> High-definition commercial product photography that captures physical textures, authentic color tonality, and structural form to turn casual store visitors into loyal buyers.

## Service Overview

Product photography at Ecomshoot is built around commercial conversion science. When prospective buyers browse online, their perception of product quality, durability, and brand prestige is dictated almost entirely by visual fidelity. Our purpose-built commercial studios combine Phase One 150MP digital backs, calibrated Broncolor Para parabolic reflectors, and precision motorized turntables to capture every angle with tactile realism.

### Drastic Return Reduction
True-to-life color calibration and millimeter-accurate texture rendering eliminate customer surprises and lower costly product returns.

## Technical Specifications

- **100+ Megapixel Medium Format Capture**: Every shot delivers incredible crop latitude and microscopic detail resolution for pinch-to-zoom capabilities.
- **Calibrated Color Profiles**: Hardware-calibrated workflow with X-Rite ColorChecker charts guaranteeing 99.8% spectral color accuracy.

## Production Workflow

### Step 1: Product Ingestion & Spec Audit
Products arrive at our secure studio, inspected under 5000K daylight bulbs, logged into inventory, and steamed/prepped.

### Step 2: Art Direction & Lighting Architecture
Our master lighting technicians construct customized light modifiers tailored specifically to your product reflectivity.

### Step 3: Tethered Capture & Live Proofing
Shooting tethered to Capture One Pro with live client web proofing links for instant real-time feedback and shot sign-off.

### Step 4: Master Retouching & Compliance QA
Dust removal, scratch elimination, color correction against physical references, and standardized web optimization.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What camera systems and lighting do you employ?
We shoot predominantly on Phase One IQ4 150MP medium format systems and Sony Alpha 61MP bodies paired with prime tilt-shift lenses and Broncolor studio strobes.

### How do we ship products to your studio?
Once you book a production run, our logistics team issues pre-addressed shipping tags and an intake packing manifest. We store items in climate-controlled lockers and safely return or donate them upon project completion.

### What turnaround times can we expect?
Standard studio turnaround is 5 to 7 business days from receipt of goods. Expedited 48-hour turnarounds are available for critical launch windows.

### Do you provide Amazon compliant white background images?
Yes, all hero assets can be delivered on pure sRGB #FFFFFF white background with 85%+ product frame occupancy matching Amazon, Walmart, and Target specifications.
