# End-to-End Professional Commercial Product Photography for Established Enterprises

> Full-service creative production combining strategic creative direction, medium format camera hardware, master lighting, set styling, and dedicated project management.

## Service Overview

Professional product photography is a strategic investment in enterprise brand equity. When global brands launch landmark products, refresh legacy catalog architectures, or roll out omnichannel advertising campaigns, they need a production partner capable of handling intricate logistics, strict visual guidelines, and uncompromising image quality. Ecomshoot provides end-to-end studio solutions for forward-thinking brand teams.

### Dedicated Senior Creative Director
A seasoned production director leads your project from concept moodboards to lighting architecture and final quality signoff.

### Enterprise Turnkey Production
We coordinate everything: shipping manifests, prep stations, prop fabrication, talent casting, proofing, and cloud archiving.

### Unmatched Commercial Quality
State-of-the-art camera systems, precision color calibration, and master retouching that satisfy billboard and print standards.

## Technical Specifications

- **Bespoke Production Roadmaps**: Every client receives a custom call sheet, minute-by-minute shooting timetable, and collaborative proofing portal.
- **Phase One 150MP Digital Backs**: The gold standard in commercial photography, delivering dynamic range, optical acuity, and crop flexibility beyond 35mm cameras.
- **Master Color Temperature Management**: Continuous spectrophotometer monitoring guaranteeing color temperatures remain locked to 5600K throughout the shoot.
- **Secure Long-Term Asset Vault**: All RAW captures and layered master PSDs archived in geo-redundant encrypted cloud storage for 3 years at no extra charge.

### Step 1: Discovery Call & Technical Scoping
We review your project objectives, brand guidelines, channel destinations, file requirements, and delivery milestones.

Our team prepares a comprehensive pre-production book outlining lighting diagrams, talent, props, and shot-by-shot schedules.

Layered master PSDs, CMYK print files, sRGB web exports, and multi-crop variations packaged and delivered via private cloud.
